Add a widget to your Home Screen

  1. Make and save a design in Quva.
  2. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the icons jiggle, then tap +.
  3. Search for Quva, choose Photo Widget and the size you made, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Touch and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose your design by name. A design’s name is followed by its size — for example, Beach · Medium.

Common questions

My widget says “Select Photo”.

A new widget starts empty. Touch and hold it, tap Edit Widget, and choose a design by name.

I changed a design, but the widget looks the same.

Open the design from the Library, make your change and tap Save widget again — the widget is refreshed when the design is saved. If the Home Screen still shows the old picture, give iOS a moment, or remove the widget and add it again.

Why can’t I change the size of a saved design?

A widget on the Home Screen has a fixed size, and a design’s name and picture are made for it. To get another size, make a new design from the same photo.

I deleted a design and its widget is empty.

Deleting a design removes its picture, so a widget that is still on your Home Screen shows an empty placeholder. Remove it, or choose another design in Edit Widget.

Where are my photos kept?

On your iPhone, in Quva’s own storage. They are never uploaded. A backup of your iPhone may include them — see the Privacy Policy.

Can I see how a widget will look on my Home Screen before I add it?

Yes. Tap Preview on Home Screen on the Create tab, or choose it from a design’s menu in the Library. It shows your widget at its real size on a stand-in Home Screen — your own wallpaper and icons will look a little different.

How do I add text or stickers?

Once a photo is in, open the Text or Stickers tool under the picture. Both are Quva Pro tools: you can try them freely, and Quva asks you to subscribe only when you save a design that uses one.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Undo and Redo are at the top right of the Create screen once a photo is in. They walk back and forward through everything you changed.

Can I start a new design from one I already made?

In the Library, open a design’s menu and tap Use as template. Create opens with its look, frame, layout, text and stickers; then choose new photos.

How do I see the introduction again?

Open Settings and tap Show the tour again, under Help.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You only need one to buy or restore Quva Pro.

What happens to my widgets if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Widgets you made keep showing their pictures. A design that uses Pro tools opens with those tools marked, and you can remove them or subscribe again.

How do I restore a purchase?

In Quva, open Settings, tap Quva Pro, then tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel Quva Pro?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Quva Pro and tap Cancel. If you subscribe, Manage Subscription in Quva’s Settings takes you there too. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Does Quva work on iPad or Mac?

Quva is made for iPhone, on iOS 18 or later.
