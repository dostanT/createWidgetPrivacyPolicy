Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version

  • Quva has no account, no ads and no analytics on how you use it.
  • The photos you pick, your designs and your widgets stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • The only network traffic Quva itself makes is Apple’s, for the Quva Pro subscription. No other company is involved; the few links you can tap open in your browser.
  • Quva uses Apple’s photo picker, so it sees only the photos you choose and never asks to read your whole library.

Who we are

Quva is an iPhone app made by Dostan Turlybek, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Quva app and this website.

If anything here is unclear, write to support@quva.example.

Where your data lives

Everything you make in Quva is stored on your iPhone:

WhatExamples
Photos you pickA copy of each photo you choose, reduced to at most 2048 pixels on its longer side, so you can edit the design later.
DesignsThe name and size of a widget, and the settings that make its picture: the position of each photo, the look, adjustments, frame, background, margin and layout, and any text and stickers you add.
Widget picturesThe finished picture of each widget, and the list of your widgets, which the Quva widget reads to show them on your Home Screen.
SettingsAppearance, accent colour, whether animations and haptics are on, and whether you have finished the introduction and which tips you have dismissed.

This information is kept in Quva’s protected storage on your iPhone, which the app shares only with its own widget. It is not sent to us or to any third party — Quva has no servers of its own to send it to.

If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup or to a computer), the backup may include this data. Those copies are held by Apple or by you, under Apple’s terms — not by us.

What we collect

Through the app: nothing about you or how you use it. We don’t collect your name, email address, contact details, location, contacts, photos or usage analytics, and Quva doesn’t ask you to create an account.

Your photos: Quva uses Apple’s photo picker. It receives only the photos you choose, and it does not ask for permission to read your photo library.

Through Apple: if you’ve chosen to share app analytics with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may make anonymous, aggregated usage and crash reports available to us through App Store Connect. We can’t identify you from them. Apple also gives us aggregated sales and subscription reports.

Through this website: nothing. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Like any web server, the service that hosts these pages may keep standard access logs — such as IP address, date and the page requested — for security and operations. When you open this website from the app, that visit is between you and this site.

Purchases

Quva is free to use. Quva Pro is an optional yearly subscription that you buy through the App Store, using Apple’s own purchase technology (StoreKit).

  • Apple processes your payment. We never see your card details, your Apple Account or your billing address.
  • Apple tells the app what you’ve bought. Quva asks Apple whether Quva Pro is active for your Apple Account, and Apple answers on your device. We run no purchase server, and no other company — no third-party purchase service — takes part.

We don’t learn your name or contact details when you subscribe. To manage or cancel a subscription, use your Apple Account settings or Manage Subscription in Quva’s Settings.

Apple features Quva uses

Quva uses several iOS features. They run on your device, and none of them sends information to us.

  • The photo picker. You choose photos in Apple’s own picker. Quva gets those photos and nothing else from your library.
  • Widgets. The Quva widget shows a finished picture on your Home Screen, using data on your device. Anyone who can see your Home Screen can see it.
  • The share sheet. If you tap Share on a design, iOS shows its share sheet with the widget’s picture. What happens next is between you and the app or person you choose.
  • The App Store. Subscriptions, restoring purchases and managing a subscription go through Apple.

Links that open outside the app

A few things in Quva are links: this website’s Privacy Policy, Terms and support page, Apple’s page for managing a subscription, and the credits for the sample photos, which lead to pages on Wikimedia Commons. Tapping one opens it in your browser or in Apple’s own interface. Quva sends nothing along with it, and what happens on that page is between you and whoever runs it — Wikimedia’s own privacy policy applies to Commons.

What we never do

  • We don’t upload your photos or your designs anywhere.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information.
  • We don’t show ads or work with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and Quva doesn’t use your advertising identifier.
  • We don’t put analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party code in the app.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting a design in Quva removes its photos, settings and picture from your iPhone and from the list of widgets; a widget that is still on your Home Screen then shows an empty placeholder. Deleting Quva removes everything it stored on that device.

Backups of your iPhone are held by Apple or by you, and stay until you delete them there.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California and other US states — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. We don’t sell or share personal information as those terms are used in California law, and we won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

Quva holds almost nothing about you: your photos and designs are on your iPhone, where you can see and delete them. If you think we hold something about you, write to us and we’ll reply as soon as we reasonably can, and within any period the law sets.

Children

Quva is a general-audience creative app and isn’t directed to children under 13, or under 16 where the law sets a higher age. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we’ll delete it.

International transfers

We don’t transfer your Quva data anywhere: it stays on your iPhone. The App Store and any backup you make are operated by Apple under its own terms.

Security

Your data lives in Quva’s protected storage on your iPhone and benefits from iOS’s built-in protections, such as the app sandbox and device encryption when you use a passcode. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping your photos on your device avoids a whole class of risks.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change would meaningfully affect how your data is handled — for example, if Quva ever starts sending anything off your device, such as syncing through iCloud or sharing to a server — we’ll update this policy before the change reaches you, and tell you in the app where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions, requests or concerns? Write to support@quva.example. The developer of Quva is Dostan Turlybek.
