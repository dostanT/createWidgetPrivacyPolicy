How it works

Three steps, no setup.

Pick

Choose one photo, or up to six, in Apple’s own photo picker. Quva never asks for access to your whole library.

Design

Choose a size, frame each photo, and add a look, a frame, a background, some text or a sticker — or start from a template. Everything previews as you go, and every change can be undone.

Place

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap +, choose Quva, then pick your design in the widget’s settings. Quva shows you the steps — and a preview of your widget on a Home Screen — right after you save.

Private by design

Your photos never leave your iPhone.

Quva has no servers. It doesn’t ask you to sign in, it shows no ads, it has no analytics and it contains no third-party code. The only network traffic it makes is Apple’s, for the subscription.

Apple’s photo picker

You choose the photos. Quva sees only those, and never asks for access to your library.

Stored on your iPhone

A copy of each photo and the finished widget picture live in Quva’s own storage on your device.

Nothing to send

There is no account, no upload and no sharing with anyone unless you choose to share a picture yourself.
